Community Learning Grant Application

Applicants must be residents of Example County and at least 18 years old on the date they apply. Applicants must submit a completed application, proof of residence, and a copy of a photo ID.

Applications must be received by October 15, 2027. The application fee is $25 and is non-refundable. Applications received after the deadline will not be reviewed.

If your application is approved, you must notify the program office within 10 days if your mailing address changes. Missing documents may delay review. Contact the Example County program office if any requirement is unclear.
